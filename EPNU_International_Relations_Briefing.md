### 4.5 International Standards Built into Academics
- **English-medium instruction** across programs, with English textbooks and exams [S7].
- **Credit-hour system**, which the site notes is used in the USA and Canada. One semester's load is defined as 25–28 contact hours/week, **750 hours of student workload ≈ 30 ECTS** [S7]. Medicine is ECTS-based [S21].
- **Mobility-ready rules already in the regulations:** non-degree registration (with a transcript), auditor registration, and transfer with course equivalency [S6, S7]. Graduation certificates are issued **in Arabic and English** [S47].
- **Entry routes in the admission rules:** **British IGCSE**, **Nile International Certificate (CNISE)**, STEM, and **foreign certificates**. Foreign certificates must be officially recognized in the issuing country and show 12 years of schooling. A **passport** is accepted as ID. **Arabic proficiency is required for non-native speakers**. English is shown by international tests (no minimum score published) or the university's own test [S8].
  - *Nuance:* the **2026–27 intake call** lists only Egyptian Thanaweya Amma, Al-Azhar, STEM and Nile Schools certificates. It says certificates not listed will not be considered, and that the university **does not deal with any agents or intermediaries inside or outside Egypt** [S48].
- **Quality:** The President is a certified NAQAAE reviewer. The university has a Quality Assurance Center [S3, S20].

## 6. Data Caveats (check before presenting)

1. **Posting date vs. event date.** Many news items carry the same date, **8 July 2026**. This is likely a batch upload, so the date may not be the event date. Affected items include the UPM "first cooperation" post, the Russian-Arab Rectors forum and the Horizon Europe invitation. Avoid exact event dates for these.
2. **Inconsistencies on the website:**
   - **Board Vice Chairman.** The homepage names Maj. Gen. Ahmed Abdullah; the Oct 2024 and Mar 2026 news name **Prof. Dr. Mohamed Hassan El-Kenawy** [S1, S23, S24].
   - **Prof. El-Sayed Hussein Hegazy.** The Board page calls him a pediatrics pioneer; the Oct 2024 news calls him an Emeritus Professor of **Engineering** at Port Said University [S4, S24].
   - **Senate role.** The President's page lists Senate membership as current; the Board page and Mar 2026 news say "**former** Senate member" [S3, S4].
   - **Board size.** The Board page says 12 members but lists 11 [S4].
   - **Academic titles differ across pages.** Dr. Sherine El-Khouly and Dr. Osama El-Baksawy appear with different titles on different pages.
3. **Program counts differ between pages.** The Programs page lists 13; the faculty pages show extra tracks (Section 3). Say "13+ programs".
4. **UPM:** the 2nd webinar (29 Apr 2026) was announced but its occurrence is not confirmed. No student or staff mobility has been reported.
5. **57357:** the website describes the university-level MoU as "under discussion" [S32], but the university's Facebook page reports it **signed**, by CEO Prof. Sherif Abouelnaga [S51a]. Treat it as signed; the website has not been updated, and Facebook post dates are hidden (item 7).
6. **Statistics:** "5,200+ students" is a website counter, not an audited figure. The founding date is not published. Campus size (44 feddans), cost (EGP 3.5 bn) and the 6 academic buildings come only from a Ministry of Higher Education report shared on Facebook, which describes an early construction phase [S51e].
7. **Facebook post dates are hidden.** Facebook does not show exact post dates in its markup, so the order of Facebook items is **inferred** from content (academic-year references, who held office, etc.). Avoid exact dates for the 57357, Ahl Masr, BEDO and PSU session 223 items [S51].
8. **Facebook titles and roles.** Facebook posts mix "Dr.", "Assoc. Prof." and "Asst. Prof." for the same people, so Section 2.3 uses "Dr." throughout. Dr. Ali Gomaa (Business Sciences) and Construction Engineering appear on Facebook only, not on the website [S51d, S51]. The Vice President for Academic Affairs is unconfirmed (Section 2.3).

1. **Title:** "East Port Said National University: Building International Bridges from the Suez Canal". Add the site's own pillar, "Global Openness".
2. **Who we are:** 7 faculties, 13+ programs, 5,200+ students; Sinai; Egypt Vision 2030 [S1, S3, S5]
3. **Why partner with EPNU:** English-medium instruction, credit hours/ECTS, AI and health focus, strategic location (Sections 1, 4.5)
4. **Leadership with international credentials:** President (PhD from the USSR, Egyptian-Japanese Initiative); Board led by a former Prime Minister [S3, S4]
5. **Academic Leadership:** program directors per faculty and the university offices, including the International Relations Office (Section 2.3) [S51, S51d]
6. **IR mandate, Mar → Jun → Jul 2026 timeline:** Minister's directives, National Universities Council priorities and international MoUs, PSU Council (Section 4.1)
7. **Flagship partner — Universiti Putra Malaysia:** signed agreement → webinar → virtual research meeting → 2nd edition announced → *next: exchange* [S12, S13]
8. **Global networks map:** Malaysia (UPM), Japan (initiative), EU (Horizon Europe), Russia–Arab world (Rectors' Union), Italy (Torino fellowship / Zohr event), UK (Exeter / Newton-Mosharafa) — each with its evidence label
9. **Global industry and academy links:** Huawei ICT Academy; Saudi German Hospital (MENA network); pharmaceutical industry partners [S9, S19, S38]
10. **People on the international stage:** internationally trained faculty; students at ICIAAI, ITC-Egypt, the International Pediatric Conference and ECPC [S9, S17, S18, S22, S44]
11. **National partnership base (one compact slide):** PSU (23 papers), ITI/Creativa, Suez Canal Authority, 57357 (MoU signed), Ahl Masr Hospital and BEDO protocols, Arish University [S51a, S51b, S51c]
12. **International readiness:** admission routes, bilingual certificates, mobility rules [S7, S8, S47]
13. **Roadmap and call to action:** give the IR Office a web page, more partners, exchange, joint degrees, international students and scholarships (Section 7)

*Tip: keep the Section 6 caveats in the speaker notes. Expect the question "how many signed international agreements?" The honest answer: one is publicly documented (UPM), and the 2026 national policy is pushing to expand.*
