## C. Agreements (upgrades to the briefing)
1. **Children's Cancer Hospital 57357 — MoU SIGNED** (the website described it only as "under discussion"). Signed under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din and **Prof. Dr. Sherif Abouelnaga, CEO of the 57357 Group**. Scope: medical education, practical training, scientific research and community service, for students of medicine, pharmacy, health sciences, biomedical engineering and computer science. A training visit took place at the signing. Witnessed by Dr. Mohamed Yasser, Dr. Mohamed El-Sakka (IR Office), Dr. Asmaa Rady and Dr. Salma Khairallah.
2. **Ahl Masr Hospital (burns) — cooperation protocol SIGNED.** Under the patronage of the President, VP Prof. Rasha Anany and **Dr. Heba El-Sewedy, Chair of the Ahl Masr Hospital Board of Trustees**. Covers clinical education, training and observation; integrating field training into the curricula; joint education and research; workshops; and capacity building. Witnessed by Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, Dr. Mohamed El-Sakka (IR Office) and the hospital's medical sector head, Dr. Ahmed Rizk.
3. **BEDO (educational-equipment and smart-systems manufacturer) — cooperation protocol SIGNED (2025).** Covers training at the company's factories, support for workshops and conferences, **joint applied research**, and support for graduation projects. Signed by BEDO CEO Dr. Mohamed Samy. A follow-up field training visit was made to the 6th of October factory.
4. **Port Said University Council, session 223:** the EPNU President took part. The agenda included strengthening international cooperation and partnerships and preparing for 2026/2027.

## D. Campus facts (Ministry of Higher Education report, shared on the page; early construction phase)
- Campus area: **44 feddans** in New Port Said city (East Port Said).
- Construction and equipment cost: **EGP 3.5 billion**.
- Buildings: an administration building and **6 academic buildings** (3 medical, 2 engineering, 1 humanities & social sciences), 2 workshop buildings, a laboratories building, an academic plaza, and recreation and student-services areas.
- The university is equipped to fourth-generation university standards.
