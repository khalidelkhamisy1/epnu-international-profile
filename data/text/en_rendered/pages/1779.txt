Student Support
Scholarships
Support and distinction opportunities for eligible and outstanding students at East Port Said National University.
Scholarships Overview
Support Based on Approved Regulations
The university offers forms of support and tuition discounts in accordance with regulations that are reviewed and updated annually after student results are approved and analyzed, then endorsed by the Board of Trustees for the new academic year.
Announced Types
Types of Support and Scholarships
Only the following types are currently published in the official regulations on the university website.
Children of Faculty Members and Administrative Staff
A 20% discount on basic tuition fees is granted to children of full-time employees only, according to the announced regulations.
Social Support Scholarships
- The University President may grant a discount to a student who loses their family provider during study, based on the family’s social circumstances, provided that the student maintains regular attendance.
- A 50% discount is granted to children of martyrs of the Armed Forces and Police, subject to submitting documentation from an accredited authority.
- A 10% sibling discount is granted when a student enrolls while their sibling is still studying at the university.
National
Application Requirements
Meeting the Announced Category
The student must fall within one of the support categories published above.
Regular Attendance
This is an announced requirement for the case of a student who loses their family provider during study.
Document Approval
An official document issued by an accredited authority is required in the case of children of martyrs.
Notice: The regulations are reviewed and updated annually. Students should refer to Student Affairs to confirm the applicable requirements before applying.

Frequently Asked Questions
Quick Answers
Are scholarship regulations fixed every year?
No. The regulations are reviewed and updated annually, then approved by the Board of Trustees.
What types of support are currently announced?
The discount for children of faculty members and administrative staff, and the social support cases listed above.
Is support available for a student who loses their family provider?
A discount may be granted according to the family’s social circumstances, provided that the student maintains regular attendance.
What is the discount rate for children of martyrs?
50% of tuition fees, subject to submitting supporting documentation from an accredited authority.
Is there a sibling discount?
Yes. A 10% discount is granted to a student whose sibling is still enrolled at the university.
What documents are required, and when does application open?
A unified list or dates have not yet been published; please refer to Student Affairs.

الرسوم الدراسية
تُنشر قيمة الرسوم المعتمدة لكل برنامج وعام دراسي من إدارة الجامعة، مع مواعيد ووسائل السداد الرسمية.
- تختلف الرسوم حسب البرنامج الأكاديمي.
- يُعتد فقط بالإعلان الرسمي الأحدث.
- توضح إدارة شؤون الطلاب مواعيد السداد والأقساط.
التحويلات
تُراجع طلبات التحويل وفق اللوائح والطاقة الاستيعابية ومعادلة المقررات.
- بيان درجات رسمي من الجهة السابقة.
- توصيف معتمد للمقررات المطلوب معادلتها.
- تحديد الساعات المقبولة بعد المراجعة الأكاديمية.
Notice: القيم والمواعيد التفصيلية تُحدّث من إدارة الجامعة بعد اعتمادها رسميًا.
