Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology
A modern faculty that prepares graduates who are academically and professionally qualified in computing and information technology through theoretical study, practical training, and academic programs aligned with the requirements of the digital age.
The Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology at East Port Said National University aims to prepare graduates who are academically and professionally qualified in computing and information technology, enabling them to contribute effectively to development and community service.
Modern Academic Education
Programs that combine scientific foundations with modern applications in computing, data, and artificial intelligence.
Practical Training
Integrating theoretical study with applied training to develop programming, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
Research and Innovation
Supporting scientific research and innovation and developing technological solutions that serve society and various sectors.
Career Readiness
Preparing graduates capable of competing locally, regionally, and internationally in modern technology careers.
