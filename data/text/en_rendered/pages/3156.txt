Program Director’s Message
We are pleased to welcome you to the Clinical Pharmacy Program, one of the important academic pathways that connects pharmaceutical sciences with practical application and aims to prepare graduates capable of understanding medicines, monitoring their safe use, and working effectively with healthcare teams.
