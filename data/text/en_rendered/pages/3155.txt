Faculty of Medicine
The faculty seeks to achieve a qualitative shift in medical education quality through an interactive learning environment that integrates modern technology, innovative medical curricula, and practical training.
Vision and Mission of the Faculty of Medicine
Modern medical education aligned with community needs, preparing competent physicians for lifelong learning and professional practice.
Faculty Vision
For the Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery Program at East Port Said National University to be a leader in medical education and scientific research, grounded in community needs and capable of graduating skilled physicians who can compete in the labor market.
Faculty Mission
To provide an educational system that graduates competent physicians capable of meeting individual and community healthcare needs locally and internationally, while supporting lifelong learning, scientific research, and community service.

About the Program Director
Assoc. Prof. Dr. Sherine El-Said El-Sayed El-Khouly leads the Medicine and Surgery Program, supporting the quality of medical education and aligning training and learning outcomes with healthcare needs and labor market requirements. Applied Medical Learning and a Clear Humanitarian Mission
This section presents the practical and humanitarian dimensions of the Medicine and Surgery Program, giving students a clearer view of their experience inside and beyond the faculty.
From Theoretical Study to Medical Practice
The program focuses on gradually building the physician’s skills through practical learning, laboratory training, and developing clinical reasoning and professional handling of medical cases.
- Early training in essential medical skills.
- Connecting medical courses with practical application.
- Developing communication skills with patients and healthcare teams.
- Preparing students to work in modern healthcare environments.
A Physician Who Learns to Serve People and Society
The Faculty of Medicine’s role is not limited to academic education; it also extends to promoting health awareness and supporting community initiatives that strengthen the university’s role in serving its surrounding environment.
- Participating in health and awareness seminars.
- Enhancing health awareness within the community.
- Developing students’ sense of responsibility and humanitarian mission.
- Linking medical education to actual community needs.
