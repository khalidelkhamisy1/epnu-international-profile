Geomatics and Artificial Intelligence Program
Overview
An interdisciplinary program that integrates geospatial sciences, geographic information systems, remote sensing, and data analysis using artificial intelligence technologies.
Program Objectives
Preparing graduates capable of collecting and analyzing spatial data, using GIS applications, employing artificial intelligence in urban and environmental planning, and supporting decision-making.
Fields of Work
Geographic information systems, digital surveying, urban planning, telecommunications companies, spatial analysis, remote sensing, smart cities, and geographic data centers.
Key Areas of Study
Field and digital surveying, geographic information systems, remote sensing, cartography, spatial databases, artificial intelligence applications, and geographic data management and analysis.
English Language and Translation Program
Overview
The program focuses on developing English language skills, written and interpreting translation, and intercultural communication.
Program Objectives
Preparing linguistically proficient graduates capable of specialized translation, professional communication, and linguistic and cultural analysis.
Fields of Work
Translation, teaching, media, language editing, tourism, public relations, international customer service, and cultural and diplomatic institutions.
Key Areas of Study
Written and interpreting translation, medical, legal, literary, and audiovisual translation, applied linguistics, discourse analysis, English literature, and research and intercultural communication skills.
