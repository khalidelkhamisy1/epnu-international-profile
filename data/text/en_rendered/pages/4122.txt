Officials, Responsibilities, and Office Duties
Each office team is presented first, followed by the official duties it undertakes in detail.
Student Affairs, Admissions, and Registration Administration
A unified system for student services from nomination and admission through graduation.
- Announcing the university admissions policy, preparing the Student Guide, and publishing it on the university website.
- Receiving lists of students nominated for admission to the university, reviewing them, and completing the required procedures.
- Organizing medical examinations and admission tests for applicants and newly admitted students.
- Receiving admission applications from new students, following up on registration, and announcing the names of accepted students.
- Registering enrolled students and following up on their paper and electronic files and records.
- Preparing records of undergraduate students at the university, including each student’s data.
- Coordinating with the Information Technology Administration to match paper records with the Admissions and Registration Information System.
- Preparing and issuing university ID cards for new and continuing students, as well as transportation subscriptions.
- Following up on transfers in accordance with applicable rules and in coordination with academic programs.
- Organizing and maintaining student files and documents related to their academic progress.
- Issuing authenticated certificates, enrollment and graduation certificates, and required statements.
- Implementing disciplinary sanctions issued against students and keeping the related decisions in their files.
- Following up on students’ military service records in coordination with the Military Service Office.
- Preparing graduate data, degree-awarding memoranda, and required statistics after results are approved.
- Preparing certificates required by external entities, handing over graduates’ documents, and completing their clearance.
- Preparing and delivering graduation certificates upon request in Arabic and English.
Student Activities Office
Supporting activities, unions, and committees and developing the student’s personality.
- Contributing to the university’s role in developing students into aware, cultured, and capable leaders.
- Preparing a comprehensive annual plan for programs, activities, schedules, and estimated budgets in consultation with the Student Union, and submitting it for approval.
- Fully supervising the implementation of the plan in cooperation with the University Student Union.
- Organizing students’ use of their free time in activities that benefit both the nation and the students themselves.
- Expanding student participation, encouraging involvement in activities and events, and helping address students’ social challenges.
- Coordinating with the University Student Union to implement cultural, social, sports, and scouting activities.
- Assisting new students in adapting positively to the university environment without affecting their academic progress.
- Preparing suitable sports programs, providing the required equipment, and supervising matches and games organized for students.
- Organizing competitions between university students and their peers at other universities.
- Participating in sports, social, cultural, and scouting events organized by other universities.
Military Service Office
Following up on students’ military service status and facilitating related procedures.
- Following up on and updating students’ military service status records.
- Reviewing required military service forms and documents and keeping them in the student’s file.
- Coordinating with the relevant authorities and the Student Affairs Administration to complete procedures on schedule.
- Providing students with guidance on study deferment, status updates, and any required documents.
