Program Details
Medical Laboratory Technology Program
About the Program
The Medical Laboratory Technology Program is one of the programs of the Faculty of Applied Health Sciences Technology. It aims to prepare qualified healthcare professionals with the scientific knowledge and practical skills required to work in medical analyses and modern laboratory technologies.
The program focuses on studying basic and medical sciences related to laboratories, such as hematology, biochemistry, microbiology, parasitology, immunology, and laboratory diagnostic techniques, with emphasis on practical training in specialized laboratories and healthcare institutions.
The program seeks to graduate specialists capable of conducting medical tests and analyses accurately and efficiently, using modern laboratory devices and technologies, and adhering to quality standards, occupational safety, and healthcare ethics to meet labor market needs in the medical sector.
Program Objectives
- Preparing qualified graduates with the scientific knowledge and applied skills required to work efficiently in medical laboratories.
- Providing students with modern scientific foundations in different fields of medical analysis, such as hematology, biochemistry, microbiology, parasitology, and immunology.
- Developing students’ skills in using modern laboratory devices and technologies and conducting laboratory tests according to quality and accuracy standards.
- Qualifying students to apply biosafety procedures and quality practices in medical laboratories.
- Developing students’ ability to analyze and interpret laboratory test results and contribute to supporting medical diagnosis.
- Enhancing scientific research and lifelong learning skills to keep pace with developments in health sciences and medical technology.
- Preparing professionals capable of competing in the healthcare labor market and contributing effectively to the development of healthcare services.
Learning Outcomes
By the end of the program, graduates will be able to:
- Understand the fundamentals of medical laboratory sciences and different fields of analysis.
- Prepare and process medical samples according to professional standards.
- Perform hematology, biochemistry, microbiology, parasitology, and immunology tests.
- Operate medical laboratory equipment and perform basic routine maintenance.
- Apply quality, occupational safety, and infection control procedures.
- Analyze laboratory test results and prepare laboratory reports.
Medical Imaging and Radiologic Technology Program
About the Program
The Medical Imaging and Radiologic Technology Program aims to prepare technology specialists capable of operating modern medical imaging technologies to assist in diagnosing medical conditions.
The program focuses on practical training using different radiology devices, while applying radiation protection and safety rules and building scientific knowledge related to diagnostic and therapeutic imaging technologies.
The program qualifies graduates to work efficiently within the healthcare team, operate various medical imaging devices safely and effectively, prepare patients for examination procedures, and adhere to quality standards in radiology units.
Program Objectives
- Preparing specialists in radiologic technology and medical imaging.
- Providing students with scientific knowledge of diagnostic and therapeutic imaging technologies.
- Training students to operate X-ray, CT, and MRI equipment.
- Enhancing skills in applying radiation protection and occupational safety rules.
- Qualifying graduates to work efficiently within the healthcare team.
Learning Outcomes
By the end of the program, graduates will be able to:
- Understanding the scientific and physical principles related to radiology and medical imaging.
- Operating different medical imaging devices efficiently and safely.
- Preparing patients for radiological examination procedures.
- Applying radiation protection rules and reducing occupational risks.
- Evaluating radiological image quality in coordination with the specialist physician.
- Adhering to quality and accreditation standards within radiology units.
Laboratories and Equipment
The program provides an educational and laboratory environment that supports the acquisition of theoretical knowledge and practical skills required by the healthcare labor market.
Specialized Laboratories
The program includes specialized teaching laboratories to train students in practical and applied aspects of health sciences technology, including medical laboratory labs equipped with the tools needed for training in different medical analyses.
Devices and Technologies
Students train in microscopy, biochemistry, fundamentals of hematology, and microbiology, in addition to training related to operating X-ray, CT, and MRI devices in the Medical Imaging and Radiologic Technology Program.
Training Environment
The program provides equipped classrooms, e-learning tools, and digital learning resources, helping students acquire theoretical knowledge and connect it with practical application.
Practical Skills
Training focuses on conducting laboratory tests, preparing samples, operating equipment efficiently and safely, preparing patients for radiological examination procedures, and applying quality and occupational safety standards.
