Faculty of Business Sciences
A contemporary learning environment that combines management sciences, accounting, marketing, and digital transformation, preparing students for decision-making and work in modern business environments. The faculty offers modern interdisciplinary programs that combine academic knowledge with practical application, supporting the preparation of graduates qualified for the labor market.
Academic Programs
Learn about the faculty’s programs, their objectives, and the key career opportunities available to graduates.
Accounting and Software Applications Program
Overview
The program combines traditional and digital accounting with financial software applications and accounting information systems.
Program Objectives
Preparing graduates capable of preparing and analyzing financial statements, using accounting software, and understanding digital transformation in the business environment.
Fields of Work
Accounting, auditing, financial analysis, taxation, banking, accounting information systems, and financial software companies.
Key Areas of Study
Financial, intermediate, and cost accounting; computerized governmental accounting; accounting information systems; financial planning and analysis; statistical analytics; and financial software applications.
Digital Marketing and E-Management Program
Overview
A program that integrates marketing and business administration concepts with digital marketing tools, e-commerce, and management through digital platforms.
Program Objectives
Preparing professionals capable of managing digital campaigns, analyzing customer behavior, managing online platforms, and developing modern marketing strategies.
Fields of Work
Digital marketing, social media management, e-commerce, market analysis, content management, digital customer service, and electronic project management.
Key Areas of Study
Digital marketing, e-commerce, platform and content management, business and data analytics, consumer behavior, e-management, and the development of digital campaigns and strategies.
