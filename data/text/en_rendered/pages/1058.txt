Innovative Engineering for a Sustainable Future
A modern engineering institution dedicated to preparing graduates capable of design, analysis, and practical application. Students begin with a foundational preparatory year, then choose their pathway among Mechatronics and Automation, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, and Energy and Electrical Power.
About the Faculty of Engineering
The Faculty of Engineering at East Port Said National University offers a modern educational model that connects core engineering knowledge with practical application and advanced technologies, preparing graduates who can think analytically, design engineering solutions, and solve real-world problems.
Academic Identity of the Faculty
Study begins with a foundational preparatory year in which students acquire the scientific and engineering foundations needed before moving into specialized pathways, ensuring the development of well-rounded engineers capable of lifelong learning and working in multidisciplinary teams.
The faculty focuses on integrating theoretical study, practical training, and applied projects, guiding students toward modern engineering fields aligned with local and regional labor market needs and the requirements of technological transformation.
Faculty Mission
Preparing engineering graduates who can employ scientific knowledge and practical skills in mechatronics and automation, artificial intelligence and machine learning, and energy and electrical power to design solutions that serve development and the labor market.
Faculty Vision
To be a distinguished learning environment in applied engineering education, innovation, and community service.
Pillars of Excellence at the Faculty of Engineering
The engineering pathway is built on four pillars that ensure applicable and developable engineering knowledge.
Educational Process
Building a strong engineering foundation in the preparatory year, then moving into precise specializations based on understanding, analysis, and problem-solving.
Practical Training
Linking study with application through field training and practical projects that help students understand the engineering work environment.
Innovation and Projects
Encouraging students to build solutions in mechatronics and automation, artificial intelligence and machine learning, and energy and electrical power that serve industry and development.
Community Engagement
Directing engineering knowledge toward projects that serve society and strengthen the university’s role in sustainable development.
Specializations That Shape the Future
The Faculty of Engineering offers three specialized programs after the preparatory year, combining applied engineering education, practical projects, and the requirements of the Fourth Industrial Revolution and digital transformation.
Mechatronics and Automation Engineering
Where mechanics meet electronics, control systems, and programming to design robotics and integrated intelligent systems.
Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineering
Your gateway to leading digital transformation through artificial intelligence tools, data analysis, and the development of intelligent models.
Energy and Electrical Power Engineering
A pathway focused on renewable energy, smart grids, energy efficiency, and sustainability in line with global trends.

Dr. Eng. Asmaa Rady Ahmed
Director of the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineering Program
Dr. Asmaa Rady serves as Director of the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineering Program at the Faculty of Engineering and also manages the university’s Huawei Academy branch.
She earned her PhD in Renewable Energy Engineering from the University of Exeter in the United Kingdom. Her research and applied interests include intelligent systems and their integration with renewable energy systems, desalination, engineering modeling, and improving the performance of engineering systems using modern methods.
She has academic experience in university teaching, academic supervision, engineering program management, curriculum and regulation development, and quality assurance in education, and has participated in several academic and research committees.
Her notable achievements include receiving the Newton-Mosharafa PhD scholarship in the United Kingdom, an international research fellowship at Politecnico di Torino in Italy, publishing internationally indexed research, and participating in locally and internationally funded applied research projects.
