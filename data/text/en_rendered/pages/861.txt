Board of Trustees
A council comprising distinguished academic, executive, and economic leaders, supporting the university’s vision and strengthening its role in education, scientific research, and community service.
Board of Trustees of East Port Said National University
The Board of Trustees plays a pivotal role in supporting the university’s strategic direction, developing its education and scientific research system, and strengthening partnerships with community and industry institutions to serve development in East Port Said and Sinai.
