TITLE: Learning Through Practice and Community Service: East Port Said National University Medical Students Conduct a Medical Convoy at Qatiya Primary Healthcare Unit
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/التعليم-بالممارسة-وخدمة-المجتمع-طلاب/
DATE: 2026-06-30T21:51:52

As part of East Port Said National University's commitment to community engagement and experiential learning, and under the patronage of **Professor Atef Al-Din**, President of East Port Said National University, and the supervision of **Dr. Sherine El-Khouly**, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program, the Faculty of Medicine organized a distinguished medical convoy at **Qatiya Primary Healthcare Unit** in North Sinai. The initiative was designed to bridge academic learning with real-world clinical practice while supporting the local community. A group of medical students participated alongside faculty members, providing a range of healthcare services, including medical examinations, health consultations, and the free distribution of medications to residents of the area. Professor Atef Al-Din emphasized that the University places great importance on practical medical education as an essential component of the learning process. He noted that organizing medical convoys reflects the University's commitment to its social responsibility and its mission to prepare highly qualified physicians equipped with both academic knowledge and clinical competence to serve their communities and contribute to the advancement of the healthcare sector. The convoy aimed to: * Improve health awareness and enhance the quality of healthcare services provided to the residents of Qatiya. * Provide medical students with valuable hands-on clinical experience while strengthening their patient communication and professional skills. * Foster the values of volunteerism, community engagement, and social responsibility among students. This medical convoy represents a successful model of integrating academic excellence with community service, offering medical students a valuable opportunity to gain practical experience while fulfilling their humanitarian mission and reinforcing the University's role as a key contributor to sustainable community development.
