TITLE: President of East Port Said National University Congratulates Egypt’s Leadership and People on the New Year 2026
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/epnu-president-new-year-2026-greetings/
DATE: 2025-12-31T10:24:00

Professor Atef Alam El-Din, President of East Port Said National University, extended his sincere congratulations to President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi and the Egyptian people on the occasion of the New Year 2026.
The University President expressed his best wishes for a year filled with prosperity and blessings for Egypt, and for continued security, stability, progress, and development.
He also congratulated the East Port Said National University community, including program directors, faculty members, teaching assistants, administrative staff, and students, wishing them a successful year of achievement, continued dedication, and academic excellence in support of the university’s mission to serve the nation.
East Port Said National University’s New Year Greetings
May God protect Egypt, its leadership and its people. Happy New Year, and may East Port Said National University continue to advance and prosper.
