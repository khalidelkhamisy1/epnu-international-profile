TITLE: Highlights from the First Introductory Seminar on Competitive Programming at Our University
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/competitive-programming/
DATE: 2024-12-04T08:29:32

Under the patronage of:
Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President,
Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President for Academic Affairs,
and organized and supervised by the University Student Welfare Department,
led by Mr. Ehab Abu El-Maati, Director of the Student Welfare Department,
and his supporting administrative team,
Today was an outstanding day full of energy and creativity with our talented university students
at the first introductory seminar on competitive programming.
The seminar covered:
- Competitive programming and its importance.
- How to get started in the field.
- How to become part of the university's competitive programming community.
We were honored by the presence of Eng. Ahmed Hossam, one of the leading experts in the field, who shared his inspiring experience and valuable advice.
Thank you to all students who attended and contributed to the success of the day.
