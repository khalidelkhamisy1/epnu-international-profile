As Part of Supporting Technological Skills, the ICPC EPNU Community Continues Preparing Students for Programming Competitions
nder the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, President of the University,
And under the supervision of Assistant Professor Dr. Osama El-Baqsawi – Director of the Computer Science Program
Within the framework of the university's support for student activities and the development of technological skills, the ICPC EPNU Community continues its efforts in qualifying and training students in problem-solving skills using programming, in order to prepare them for participation in the Egyptian University Programming Contest.
Today witnessed the organization of a programming contest within the university, where students participated in a strong competition based on teamwork, forming teams of three students who collaborate to solve a set of programming problems in the shortest possible time with the highest efficiency, submitting solutions via the designated platform, and receiving an 'Accepted' status for each correct solution, in an atmosphere full of enthusiasm and challenge.
The ICPC EPNU Community team organized this event,
under the leadership  Eng. Mohamed Osama El-Araby – Community leader
In this context, Prof. Dr. Atef Alam Al-Din, the University President, praised the efforts of the students and those in charge of this community, affirming that such activities represent a fundamental pillar in building an outstanding generation capable of creativity and competition in the fields of modern technology. He pointed out the university's constant keenness to support all initiatives that contribute to developing students' abilities and preparing them for the labor market.
