TITLE: Refresher Course Held at the University with Members of the Student Welfare Department
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/refresher-course-held-at-the-university-with-student-sponsorship/
DATE: 2024-10-30T10:42:52

Today, Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, inspected
and Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President for Academic Affairs,
the refresher course held at the university with members of the Student Welfare Department, led by Captain Ehab Abu El-Maati, to select the university team for various activities. He also joined the students in some activities.
He also listened to some suggestions submitted by students regarding the progress of the educational process at the university.
