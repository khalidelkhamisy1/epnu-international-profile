During a full training day, students underwent a realistic practical experience, where they were introduced to various workshop equipment and engaged with the industrial work environment under the on-site supervision of the center’s trainers and with oversight from:
Dr. Mohamed El-Sakka – Director of the Mechatronics and Automation Engineering Program
Dr. Asmaa Rady – Director of the Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning Engineering Program and Construction Engineering Program
Eng. Lujaina Farag – Teaching Assistant at the Faculty
Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, President of the University, emphasized that this type of practical training represents a fundamental pillar in building students’ capabilities, noting the university’s commitment to providing applied educational experiences that align with labor market requirements and contribute to preparing qualified graduates who possess both skills and knowledge.
The university extends its sincere thanks and appreciation to the Suez Canal Authority Training Center,
Major General Walid Mostafa Awad – Director of the Training Center, and Eng. Ahmed Anany for their kind cooperation, excellent organization, and warm reception.
