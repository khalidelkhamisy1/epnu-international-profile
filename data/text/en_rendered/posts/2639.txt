TITLE: Strengthening the health Care quality system: The Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program at East Port Said National University obtains the GAHAR diploma with distinction
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/تعزيزًا-لمنظومة-الجودة-الصحية-مدير-بر/
DATE: 2026-04-07T16:17:41

As part of East Port Said National University's support for academic excellence and continuous professional development, Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, extended his sincere congratulations and best wishes to Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program, on receiving the Professional Diploma in the Applications of the General Authority for Healthcare Accreditation and Regulation (GAHAR) with an overall grade of Excellent, as part of the first cohort.
This scientific achievement is a distinguished step that reflects her competence and commitment to continuous development, contributing to strengthening quality assurance and accreditation within the healthcare sector and promoting the application of best practices in delivering medical services in accordance with approved standards.
The University President affirmed that this distinction represents a qualitative addition to the university and embodies its commitment to investing in qualified academic and medical professionals, in line with the Egyptian state's direction toward improving the quality of healthcare services and achieving institutional accreditation standards.
He also commended the prominent role of the Faculty of Medicine at Port Said University, under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Sherif Saleh, University President; Prof. Dr. Ehab El-Hanafy, Dean of the Faculty of Medicine; and Prof. Dr. Heba Youssef, Supervisor of the Health Sector and Vice Dean for Graduate Studies, in offering specialized professional programs that contribute to developing medical professionals and improving the quality of healthcare in medical institutions.
The University President also expressed his sincere wishes to Dr. Sherine El-Khouly for continued success and further scientific and professional achievements.
