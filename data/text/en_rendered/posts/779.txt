TITLE: Dr. Rasha Ibrahim Anany Appointed Supervisor of the Education and Student Affairs Sector
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/الدكتور-رشا-إبراهيم-عنانى-مشرفا-على-قط/
DATE: 2024-10-17T11:17:38

The Board of Trustees of East Port Said National University, chaired by Eng. Ibrahim Mahlab, former Prime Minister of Egypt, and Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, extend their sincere congratulations
to Prof. Dr. Rasha Ibrahim Anany on her assignment to supervise the university's Education and Student Affairs Sector, in addition to her role as Director of the university's Quality and Academic Accreditation Center.
They wish her continued success and achievement.
