TITLE: A Journey into the Depths of Egypt's Cultures
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/رحلة-إلى-أعماق-ثقافات-مصر/
DATE: 2024-12-17T10:13:19

As part of a journey into the depth of Egypt's cultures, and in the presence of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President; Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President for Academic Affairs; and Mr. Moheb Fathy Heiba, Secretary-General of the University, and under the supervision of the University Student Union, represented by student Essam Gomaa, Union President, student Ahmed Zamel, Vice President of the Union, and the Student Welfare Department led by Mr. Ehab Abu El-Maati,
the university held Egyptian Cultures Day, which aims to introduce students to the customs, traditions, dialects, and distinctive foods of all governorates of Egypt. Students enjoyed presentations on historical figures and archaeological sites in each governorate, accompanied by detailed explanations from representatives of each region. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din praised the organization, addressed the students, and encouraged them to prepare for their midyear examinations, affirming that the university will make every effort to provide a healthy environment for them and wishing them continued success.
