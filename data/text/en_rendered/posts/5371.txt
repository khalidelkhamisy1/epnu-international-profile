TITLE: National Media Authority Delegation Visits East Port Said National University to Learn About Its Educational Vision and the Future of Its Academic Programs
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/وفد-من-الهيئة-الوطنية-للإعلام-في-ضيافة/
DATE: 2026-09-10T13:35:24

Prof. Dr. Atef Alam Eldin, President of East Port Said National University, welcomed Ms. Olfat Mostafa El-Sayed, Senior Broadcaster at the National Media Authority of Egypt, and her accompanying delegation at the University’s campus.
الأستاذة/ منه الله ممدوح – رئيس صفحة أخبار التعليم بجريدة أخبار اليوم.
Mr. Ayman Attia Mohamed – Head of the Sawt Al Arab Radio Network.
Mr. Salah Hussein – Head of Governance at the National Media Authority.
The meeting was attended by Ms. Ghada Shazly, Secretary-General of the University, along with the Directors of the University’s academic programs.
The meeting featured a fruitful discussion on various aspects of the University’s educational and academic activities, as well as the advanced educational system offered through its faculties and programs. Discussions also addressed the University’s vision for preparing graduates who are capable of competing in the labor market, supporting innovation and entrepreneurship, and strengthening the University’s role in serving the community.
Following the meeting, Mr. Ahmed El-Qasrawy, Director of Public Relations and Marketing at the University, accompanied the media delegation on an inspection tour of the campus, including a number of the University’s faculties and academic programs.
The delegation was welcomed by the Program Directors, who provided detailed presentations on their respective programs, including their academic structure, study format, key student activities and projects, as well as the programs’ efforts to serve the community and align academic learning with labor market needs.
The tour also included the Central Student Affairs Administration and the Parents’ Waiting Hall, where the delegation learned about the coordination and admission procedures for new students, as well as the processes followed throughout the application and admission stages. This reflects the University’s commitment to providing integrated administrative services and creating a convenient and supportive environment for students and their parents.
During the tour, the delegation viewed a selection of student projects and academic and practical activities, highlighting the University’s commitment to developing students’ academic, practical, and creative skills, while contributing to their overall personal development.
The delegation expressed its appreciation and admiration for what they witnessed at the University, praising its clear vision and integrated educational system, as well as its genuine commitment to supporting students and preparing them for the future.
The guests also commended the University’s facilities and equipment, the distinguished level of its academic programs, its attention to students and parents, and the positive atmosphere they experienced across various sectors of the University. They emphasized that what they witnessed reflects serious and tangible steps toward building a modern university that keeps pace with the requirements of the future.
