TITLE: East Port Said National University Warns Against Unofficial Admission and Transfer Channels
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/epnu-official-admission-transfer-channels/
DATE: 2025-12-30T10:17:00

East Port Said National University confirmed that all approved information regarding admission and transfer procedures is issued exclusively through the university’s premises and official channels.
The university clarified that announcements or information circulated by external parties do not represent it and should not be considered official. It stressed the importance of relying on official sources when inquiring about admission, application, and transfer procedures.
The university also urged students and parents to verify information carefully and communicate directly with the relevant university departments to obtain accurate guidance and avoid unreliable information or practices that could harm applicants’ interests.
Official Channels
Official matters concerning applications and transfers are handled only through East Port Said National University’s premises or the university’s announced official digital channels.
