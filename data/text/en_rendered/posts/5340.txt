TITLE: A New Achievement for East Port Said National University Students in the ECPC
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/إنجاز-جديد-لطلاب-جامعة-شرق-بورسعيد-الأ/
DATE: 2026-08-26T13:03:47

Under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam Eldin, President of East Port Said National University, the University continues to celebrate the achievements and outstanding participation of its students in various scientific and technological competitions and events. In a new achievement, a team from the Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology has successfully qualified for the Egyptian Collegiate Programming Contest (ECPC), reflecting the students' capabilities and skills and their ability to compete at a high level.
This qualification highlights the outstanding abilities of the Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology students in programming, problem-solving, and logical thinking. It also reflects the Faculty's commitment to developing students' practical and applied skills and encouraging them to participate in specialized competitions that help identify their potential, enhance their expertise, and prepare them for the demands of the labor market.
The team participated under the supervision of Dr. Osama El-Baksawy, Assistant Professor and Director of the Computer Science Programme, as part of the Programme's commitment to supporting outstanding students, developing their skills in programming and technology, and providing them with opportunities to compete and gain valuable experience through participation in specialized competitions
This achievement further demonstrates East Port Said National University's commitment to providing a stimulating educational environment that goes beyond academic learning to develop students' skills and competitive abilities. It also encourages them to transform the knowledge they acquire in the classroom into practical skills and applications that enable them to compete effectively and achieve outstanding results.
he University continues to support its outstanding students and encourage their participation in various scientific and technological competitions and events, contributing to the preparation of a generation capable of keeping pace with the rapid developments in programming and technology, while representing the University with distinction at various national and international forums.
