TITLE: East Port Said National University Congratulates Ms. Ghada Shazly on Her Appointment as Secretary-General
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/قرار-بتعيين-الأستاذة-غادة-شاذلي-أمينً/
DATE: 2026-07-01T22:36:42

Port Said National University, under the leadership of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam Eldin, President of the University, and Prof. Dr. Sherif Youssef Saleh, President of Port Said University, extends its sincere congratulations and best wishes to Ms. Ghada Shazly on her appointment as Secretary-General of East Port Said National University, pursuant to the decision issued by Eng. Ibrahim Mahlab, Former Prime Minister of Egypt and Chairman of the Board of Trustees. The University wishes Ms. Shazly every success in her new role and is confident that her extensive administrative experience and distinguished professional competence will contribute significantly to advancing the University's institutional development, enhancing the quality of administrative performance, and achieving its vision and mission of delivering distinguished higher education and serving the community. The University extends its warmest congratulations and best wishes for her continued success, excellence, and distinguished service to the University and the nation.
