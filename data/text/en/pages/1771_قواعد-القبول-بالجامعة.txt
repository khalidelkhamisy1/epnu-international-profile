Eligibility and Application Requirements
General Admission Requirements
The university organizes admission rules to ensure equal opportunities for all applicants who meet the required application conditions.
✓
Clear and Published Criteria
The university announces
available programs
application procedures, steps, and key dates through the university website.
Applicants must submit a certificate of completion of pre-university education, such as the Egyptian General Secondary Certificate or its equivalent.
If a foreign certificate is submitted, it must be valid and officially recognized in the issuing country.
The published requirements include the minimum admission score for each program according to the type of certificate.

Application Procedures
Application and Coordination Procedures
Online Application
Applications are submitted through the university website, with scanned copies of the required documents attached.
Data Review
The university reviews applicants’ information and verifies that the announced requirements and minimum admission scores are met.
Nomination Notification
An email is sent to the student nominated for admission to complete the procedures for submitting original documents.
Notification of Ineligible Applicants
Students who do not meet the minimum score or requirements are notified of the rejection of their nomination as soon as possible.
Final Admission
Final University Admission Procedures
The
Education and Student Affairs Administration
reviews the student’s documents to determine eligibility for admission to the selected preference.
If the student’s documents do not comply with the announced requirements, the nomination will be cancelled.
The university provides early academic advising for every admitted student.
The university may conduct placement tests in language, digital skills, and critical thinking when required.
The student meets with the academic advisor to agree on the study plan and determine first-semester courses.
After Admission
Key University Study Regulations
A summary of the key rules stated in program regulations. Each student should contact the university directly to confirm details related to their program and individual status before completing enrollment.
01
Academic System
The credit-hour system generally applies, except for Medicine programs, which follow an integrated system. The academic year consists of fall and spring semesters, in addition to a summer semester.
02
Language Requirements
Passing the English language requirement is a condition for admission. Arabic language proficiency is required for non-native speakers, with equivalent certificates accepted as announced by the university.
03
Attendance
Attendance of at least 75% of course hours is required to be eligible to sit for the final examination.
04
Passing Requirements
Students must achieve at least 60% of the total course grade and at least 30% of the final examination grade.
05
Fees and Registration
Registration and educational service fees are paid at the start of registration in accordance with the relevant decisions and regulations.
06
Academic Follow-Up
An academic warning begins when the semester GPA falls below 1.7. A bachelor’s degree is awarded only with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0 after graduation requirements are fulfilled.
!
Important Notice
Students must contact the university directly to inquire about any additional details or requirements related to their individual status or chosen program.
Frequently Asked Questions
Important Notes
Is the application submitted online?
Yes. Applications are submitted through the university website, with scanned copies of the required documents attached.
When should original documents be submitted?
Original documents are submitted after admission is completed and in accordance with the instructions sent to the student.
How will applicants be contacted?
Applicants are contacted by email throughout all stages of the process.
Are there placement tests?
The university may conduct placement tests in the required skills before or during the first year.
1. Student Admission Policy
The university announces all programs approved to begin study. Application procedures, steps, and relevant dates are also published on the university website. The university ensures equal opportunities for all applicants who meet the required application conditions.
Applicants must submit a certificate of completion of pre-university education, such as the Egyptian General Secondary Certificate. If a foreign certificate is submitted, it must be valid, recognized in the issuing country, and allow the applicant to enroll in universities in that country.
The published requirements include the minimum admission score for each program according to the type of certificate.
Applications are submitted through the university website, with photocopies of all required documents attached.
Applicants are contacted by email throughout all stages of the process.
2. Program Application Procedures
Applications to the program are open to holders of the Egyptian General Secondary Certificate from the current academic year or an equivalent certificate, provided that all application requirements are met.
Applications for university admission are open to all students holding the British IGCSE certificate and the Nile International Certificate (CNISE) who are enrolled in Grade 12 before their final results are issued, provided that they complete their documents immediately after final results are released.
Clear photocopies of the following documents must be attached to the application form. The original documents must be submitted after admission to the university is completed:
A passport-size personal photo with a white background.
Birth certificate, national ID card, or passport. Egyptian male applicants must submit their national ID number.
Secondary school completion certificate or transcript, or their equivalent from other certificates.
Students holding foreign certificates must submit proof of completing twelve (12) years of schooling.
Proof of passing international English language examinations, if available, as follows: the minimum TOEFL or IELTS score (... reading - ... writing - ... listening). If unavailable, the applicant must pass the university’s English language examination.
3. Coordination Procedures
An email is sent to the student nominated for admission, requesting submission of original certificates and admission requirements to Student Affairs at the faculty to which the student has been nominated.
Students who do not meet the minimum admission requirement will be notified of the rejection of their nomination as soon as possible.
4. Final University Admission Procedures
The student’s documents are reviewed by the university’s Education and Student Affairs Administration to determine whether the student can be admitted to the preference selected.
If the student’s documents do not comply with the announced requirements, the nomination will be cancelled and a place will become available in the nominated program.
The university provides an additional early academic advising service through a meeting with each admitted student to ensure their ability to adapt to the program for which they have been nominated.
The university requires a specific language level for its students, sufficient knowledge of digital learning tools, and relevant certificates. It also supports students through critical thinking courses. The university confirms that admitted students will take placement tests in these skills, and students who do not meet the required level will receive courses during the first year without affecting their enrollment or registration in first-level courses.
The student then meets with the academic advisor to agree on the study plan and determine the courses to be registered in the first semester.
