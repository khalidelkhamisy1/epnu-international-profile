TITLE: East Port Said National University Monitors First-Semester Examinations in Medicine and Pharmacy
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/epnu-first-semester-exams-medicine-pharmacy/
DATE: 2026-01-01T01:44:00

East Port Said National University continues to monitor first-semester examinations for students in the Clinical Pharmacy and Medicine and Surgery programs, with calm, disciplined conditions and organizational measures ensuring the smooth operation of examination halls.
The examinations are being held under the patronage of Professor Atef Alam El-Din, University President, and the supervision of Associate Professor Mohamed Yasser, Director of the Clinical Pharmacy Program, and Dr. Sherine El-Kholy, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program.
During his tour of the examination halls, the University President emphasized the university’s commitment to providing a safe and well-organized examination environment that serves students’ interests and ensures full compliance with regulations. He noted that the orderly conduct of examinations reflects the university’s readiness and coordination.
Monitoring Medicine and Pharmacy Examination Halls
Associate Professor Mohamed Yasser monitored the Clinical Pharmacy examination halls and verified compliance with the approved instructions, while Dr. Sherine El-Kholy followed up on the Medicine and Surgery examinations and ensured that students were provided with suitable conditions.
The examinations reflected effective cooperation among faculty members, teaching assistants, and administrative staff, demonstrating the spirit of teamwork across the university. East Port Said National University wishes all its students continued success and excellence.
