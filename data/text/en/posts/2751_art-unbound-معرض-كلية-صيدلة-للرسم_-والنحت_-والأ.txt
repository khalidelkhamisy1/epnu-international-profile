nder the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, President of the University,
and under the supervision of Asst. Prof. Dr. Mohamed Yasser Hamdy, Director of the Clinical Pharmacy Program,
and under the supervision of the Student Activities Office,
Students of the Artistic and Cultural Committee, in collaboration with the Students for Egypt student family at the Faculty of Pharmacy, organized outstanding events that combined creativity and cultural engagement through two prominent exhibitions:
Art Unbound
n a vibrant and lively atmosphere, our students showcased a diverse collection of artistic works, including paintings, sculptures, and handicrafts, presented at a high level that reflects a distinguished artistic sense and a promising creative vision. It highlights a generation equipped with the tools of expression, confidently conveying its identity through art.
Book Hub
This exhibition came as a student-led initiative based on self-driven efforts, where Faculty of Pharmacy students shared their personal books in a rich experience of knowledge and cultural exchange. The exhibition reflects a genuine awareness of the value of reading and its role in shaping minds, within a framework that supports sustainability and fosters a spirit of innovation.
n this context, Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din praised the remarkable level of organization of both exhibitions, emphasizing that such activities reflect the university’s commitment to building well-rounded students who combine academic excellence with cultural awareness and creativity. He also noted that supporting student talents is at the core of the university’s strategy to prepare a generation capable of innovation and active contribution to community development.
The events reflected a remarkable passion among students, affirming that we have a conscious generation that recognizes the importance of culture alongside academic specialization—a generation we take pride in and strive to graduate not only academically qualified, but also cultured and creative, capable of making a real difference.
