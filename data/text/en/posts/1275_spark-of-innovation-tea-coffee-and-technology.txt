TITLE: “Spark of Innovation: Tea, Coffee, and Technology”

DATE: 2024-12-18T10:20:31

Under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, and Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President for Academic Affairs,
the Mechatronics and Automation Engineering Program organized
a distinguished student event entitled:
“Spark of Innovation: Tea, Coffee, and Technology”
In an atmosphere full of enthusiasm and creativity, students and faculty members gathered to discuss a range of innovative ideas and projects in mechatronics and industrial applications of automated systems.
During the event, Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany discussed the students' work as they presented their distinguished practical projects, which included:
A robot that follows lines drawn on the ground.
Car-shaped robots controlled remotely via Bluetooth.
Miniature models of industrial robots: one with four axes and another with six axes.
This event was not merely a project exhibition; it was an opportunity for exchanging ideas and fostering collaboration between students and faculty members, strengthening the spirit of innovation in an inspiring educational environment.
