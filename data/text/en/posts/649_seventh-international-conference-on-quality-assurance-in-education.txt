TITLE: Proceedings of the Seventh International Conference Organized by the National Authority for Quality Assurance and Accreditation of Education
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/seventh-international-conference-on-quality-assurance-in-education/
DATE: 2024-11-04T10:28:55

Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, participated
in the proceedings of the Seventh International Conference organized by the National Authority for Quality Assurance and Accreditation of Education, which was inaugurated by
Prof. Dr. Ayman Ashour, Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research, on behalf of Dr. Mostafa Madbouly, Prime Minister. This year's conference was held under the theme "Quality of Education in the Age of Artificial Intelligence".
The conference was held in the presence of Prof. Dr. Mohamed El-Duwaini, Deputy of Al-Azhar, representing His Eminence the Grand Imam Prof. Dr. Ahmed El-Tayeb, Sheikh of Al-Azhar.
It was also attended by Minister Mohamed Abdel Latif, Minister of Education and Technical Education.
A number of ministers, Egyptian university presidents, education quality experts, and stakeholders also attended.
