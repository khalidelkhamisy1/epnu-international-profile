TITLE: Our First Steps toward Launching Entrepreneurship and Innovation Activities at East Port Said National University
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/entrepreneurship-and-innovation/
DATE: 2024-10-30T10:36:54

Based on our university's belief in its students' capabilities and the importance of supporting their ambitions, and in line with the university's vision to prepare a generation of leaders and innovators who will contribute to building Egypt's future,
we are pleased
to announce our first steps toward launching entrepreneurship and innovation activities at East Port Said National University.
EPU Innovators
Under the generous patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President,
and with the support of Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President for Academic Affairs,
and under the supervision of Assoc. Prof. Dr. Mohamed Yasser, the university's Entrepreneurship and Innovation Coordinator,
EPU Innovators
this platform brings together knowledge and ambition and gives university students the opportunity to develop their entrepreneurship skills through a practical and inspiring experience.
EPU Innovators
It is a forum for ideas and ambitions, aimed at transforming your ideas into successful projects that add real value to you and to our community. It will also serve as an umbrella bringing together all student families and entities, providing an ideal space for generating ideas and innovation, and offering workshops, training courses, and opportunities to connect with entrepreneurship experts to support you and expand your professional horizons.
If you aspire to join this distinctive journey, you can register through the following link:
https://docs.google.com/…/1FAIpQLScGmePcPXy…/viewform…
We look forward to your ideas and creativity so that together we can shape the future of entrepreneurship at our university.
