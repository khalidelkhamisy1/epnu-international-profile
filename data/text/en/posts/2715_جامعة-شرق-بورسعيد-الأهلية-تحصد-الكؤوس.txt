East Port Said National University Secures Top Positions at the Third Sports Day under the Slogan “Sinai First”
East Port Said National University participated in the Third Sports Day organized by Sinai University, Arish Branch, in a highly competitive atmosphere that reflected the spirit of enthusiasm and cooperation among students from participating universities.
The university achieved remarkable results, as follows:
Five-a-side Football: Third Place
3x3 Basketball (Men): Third Place
3x3 Basketball (Women): Third Place
At the conclusion of the event, Prof. Dr. Abdel Hamid Agwa, Vice President of Sinai University for Education and Student Affairs, honored the participating delegations, where commemorative shields were presented to the heads of delegations, and medals and trophies were awarded to the teams that secured the top three positions.
Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, President of the University, affirmed that these results reflect the spirit of determination and belonging among East Port Said National University students, praising the distinguished efforts of the Student Activities Office in supporting various activities and providing a motivating environment that contributes to building well-rounded students and developing their skills.
He also emphasized the university’s continued full support for all student activities, believing in their role in developing students’ abilities and promoting the spirit of competition and teamwork.
