TITLE: As Part of Its Efforts to Develop Students' Skills, East Port Said National University Organizes a First Aid Training Workshop
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/بناء-مهارات-وإنقاذ-الأرواح-جامعة-شرق-ب/
DATE: 2026-06-30T22:12:37

في إطار دعم الأنشطة الطلابية والفعاليات الهادفة لتنمية مهارات طلاب جامعة شرق بورسعيد الأهلية، نُظمت دورة تثقيفية مميزة في الإسعافات الأولية،
وذلك تحت رعاية الأستاذ الدكتور/ عاطف علم الدين – رئيس الجامعة،
وإشراف الدكتورة/ شيرين الخولي – مدير برنامج الطب والجراحة.
As part of its commitment to supporting student activities and providing meaningful educational experiences that enhance students' practical skills, **East Port Said National University** organized a distinguished **First Aid Training Workshop** under the patronage of **Professor Atef Al-Din**, President of the University, and the supervision of **Dr. Sherine El-Khouly**, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program.
The workshop was delivered by **Dr. Osama Khalil**, Lecturer at the Faculty of Medicine, Al-Azhar University, who presented a comprehensive and interactive session covering the essential principles and life-saving techniques of first aid. Through practical demonstrations and simplified explanations, participants learned how to respond effectively to emergency situations and provide appropriate initial care before professional medical assistance arrives.
The event was exceptionally organized by students of the Faculty of Medicine, whose dedication, teamwork, and professionalism contributed significantly to the success of the workshop, reflecting their strong sense of responsibility and commitment.
The workshop aimed to raise students' medical awareness, equip them with essential first aid skills, and encourage them to acquire knowledge that could one day help save lives.
At the conclusion of the event, East Port Said National University reaffirmed its commitment to supporting educational and training initiatives that prepare competent, knowledgeable, and socially responsible graduates capable of serving their communities with confidence and professionalism.
The University extends its sincere appreciation to everyone who contributed to the success of this valuable and impactful event.

✨ تهدف هذه الدورة إلى رفع الوعي الطبي لدى الطلاب، وتشجيعهم على اكتساب مهارات قد تكون سببًا في إنقاذ حياة إنسان.
وفي ختام الفعالية، تؤكد جامعة شرق بورسعيد الأهلية استمرارها في دعم الأنشطة التعليمية والتدريبية التي تسهم في بناء طالب قادر، واعٍ، ومؤهل لخدمة المجتمع بمهارة واقتدار.
