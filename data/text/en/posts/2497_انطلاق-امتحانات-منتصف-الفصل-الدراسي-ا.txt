TITLE: Midterm Examinations for the Second Semester of the 2025/2026 Academic Year Begin at East Port Said National University
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/انطلاق-امتحانات-منتصف-الفصل-الدراسي-ا/
DATE: 2026-03-30T16:24:21

As part of East Port Said National University's commitment to the regularity of the educational process and the enhancement of academic performance quality, midterm examinations for the second semester began on Sunday, March 29, 2026, across the various academic programs.
The examinations were held in five academic programs. Examination committees witnessed notable student attendance and a disciplined, well-organized atmosphere, with all necessary means of comfort and support provided to ensure that examinations are conducted in an appropriate educational environment.
The examinations are held under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, who emphasized the importance of adhering to the regulations governing examination procedures and of creating the appropriate atmosphere to ensure equal opportunities among students.
He also directed the relevant departments to continuously follow up on examination procedures to ensure the regularity of committees and the immediate handling of any obstacles that may arise, thereby ensuring the efficient and orderly conduct of the examination process.
For their part, the various program administrations were keen to apply all organizational procedures, adhere to approved timetables, and provide qualified academic and administrative staff to supervise the examination committees.
