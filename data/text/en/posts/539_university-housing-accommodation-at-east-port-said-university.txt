TITLE: Intensive Efforts to Establish University Housing at East Port Said National University
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/university-housing-accommodation-at-east-port-said-university/
DATE: 2024-11-23T09:21:27

As part of East Port Said National University's efforts to achieve integration across all aspects of university life and ensure stability for its students,
Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, and Mr. Moheb Fathy Heiba, Secretary-General of the University,
visited Major General Tarek Gamal, Head of the North Sinai Development Authority,
to request the allocation of a suitable plot of land adjacent to the university for establishing a university residence complex,
including accommodation for male students, female students, and administrative housing for faculty members. An official request has been submitted,
and the university is following up on the required procedures for land allocation and the start of construction of the university residence complex.
