TITLE: Port Said East Private University mourns the wife of the university president
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/جامعة-شرق-بورسعيد-الأهلية-تنعى-زوجة-رئ/
DATE: 2026-04-21T12:25:23

East Port Said National University and its Board of Trustees, chaired by Eng. Ibrahim Mahlab, former Prime Minister of Egypt,
together with the university's academic and administrative staff and students, extend their sincere condolences and heartfelt sympathy on the passing of the late,
Mrs. Hanan Ahmed Auf Hamada, wife of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President,
who has passed away.
The university community extends its deepest condolences to Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din and the family of the deceased,
praying that Almighty God grant her His vast mercy and admit her to the highest ranks of Paradise,
and grant her family patience and solace.
