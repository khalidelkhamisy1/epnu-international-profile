TITLE: Preparing to Participate in the Ibdaa 13 Competition
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/creativity-13/
DATE: 2024-11-12T10:00:35

Today, Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, inspected
and Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President for Education and Student Affairs, attended the preparatory meeting for the theatre and musical theatre teams at the university theatre.
The meeting was held under the patronage of the Student Welfare Department and in the presence of director Mohamed El-Malky and his supporting team.
This came in preparation for participation in the Ibdaa 13 Competition organized by the Ministry of Youth and Sports.
The competition is held under the patronage of H.E. President Abdel Fattah El-Sisi.
He praised the students' performance and affirmed the university's full support for all talents across all faculties.
He wished them continued success and achievement.
