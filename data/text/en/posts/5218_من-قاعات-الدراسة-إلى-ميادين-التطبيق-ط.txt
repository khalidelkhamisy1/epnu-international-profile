TITLE: From classrooms to the fields of application… Students of East Port Said National University continue to gain experience from leading national institutions.
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/من-قاعات-الدراسة-إلى-ميادين-التطبيق-ط/
DATE: 2026-07-28T09:55:10

Under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam Eldin, President of East Port Said National University, and the supervision of Assistant Professor Dr. Osama El-Baksawy, Director of the Computer Science Program, the Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology organized an educational visit to the Armed Forces Institute of Information Systems, reflecting the University's commitment to providing a comprehensive educational experience that integrates academic knowledge with practical application.
During the visit, students explored the Institute's advanced educational and training facilities. As one of Egypt's leading institutions specializing in information technology, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and digital transformation, the Institute plays a significant role in preparing highly qualified professionals capable of keeping pace with the rapid advancements in the technology sector.
The visit included an introductory presentation highlighting the Institute's mission and capabilities, followed by a guided tour of its facilities, including the Military Hall. Students also attended a specialized lecture entitled "Artificial Intelligence (AI)", which explored the latest applications of artificial intelligence, its future across various sectors, and its vital role in supporting digital transformation.
Commenting on the visit, Prof. Dr. Atef Alam Eldin, President of the University, emphasized that true education extends beyond the classroom to direct engagement with leading national institutions and learning from their expertise. He noted that such visits play a vital role in developing students' practical skills, broadening their professional perspectives, and preparing them to become highly qualified graduates capable of competing successfully and contributing to Egypt's digital future.
East Port Said National University remains committed to its vision of preparing graduates equipped with knowledge, practical skills, and professional experience by expanding collaboration with leading national institutions. Through such partnerships, the University continues to enhance the quality of education, foster innovation, and align academic outcomes with the evolving needs of the labor market.
