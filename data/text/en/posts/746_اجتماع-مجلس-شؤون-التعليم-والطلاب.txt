The meeting was held under the chairmanship of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President,
with the membership of:
Prof. Dr. Rasha Ibrahim Anany, Supervisor of the Education and Student Affairs Sector,
Prof. Dr. El-Sayed Galal, Director of the Faculty of Engineering Programs,
Prof. Dr. Rasha Emad, Director of the Laboratory Program at the Faculty of Health Sciences Technology,
Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program,
The University Secretary-General, Mr. Moheb Fathy Heiba,
Director of Central Student Affairs at the University, Mr. Mohamed Zangir,
Director of Financial Affairs, Ms. Mayada El-Far,
Information Technology Officer, Mr. Tayser El-Sayed,
The University President presented the achievements made during the period from September 28 and the start of the academic year to date, praising the efforts made by all parties.
The meeting also discussed several academic and administrative topics, the level of readiness for midterm examinations, the preparedness of examination control units, and the related procedures.
