TITLE: Former Prime Minister Eng. Ibrahim Mahlab and Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din Congratulate the University Community and the Islamic Nation on Eid Al-Fitr
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/رئيس-مجلس-الوزراء-الأسبق-المهندس-إبرا/
DATE: 2026-03-19T10:54:06

With sentiments filled with affection and pride,
the University Board of Trustees, chaired by Eng. Ibrahim Mahlab, former Prime Minister and Chairman of the Board of Trustees, and Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President and former member of the Senate,
extend their highest congratulations and best wishes to all members of the university community, including faculty members, teaching assistants, administrative staff, and our students, on the occasion of Eid Al-Fitr.
As the University Presidency and its Board of Trustees mark this blessed occasion, they express their sincere appreciation for the efforts of all dedicated university personnel in advancing the path of knowledge and development. They also extend heartfelt congratulations to the entire Islamic nation, praying that Almighty God accept our good deeds and yours, return these blessed days to our dear homeland and the whole world with goodness, prosperity, and blessings, and preserve security, stability, and prosperity for our beloved Egypt under its wise leadership. Eid Mubarak.
