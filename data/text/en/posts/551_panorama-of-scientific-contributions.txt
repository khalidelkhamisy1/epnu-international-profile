TITLE: A Panorama of University Students' Scientific Contributions in Conferences, Events, and Educational, Research, and Community Initiatives
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/panorama-of-scientific-contributions/
DATE: 2024-11-22T09:25:55

Under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President,
and Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President for Academic Affairs,
we present a panorama of university students' scientific contributions in conferences, events, and educational, research, and community initiatives
October - November 2024
through which the university advances its most important objective, derived from the National Strategy for Higher Education and Scientific Research: building competitive competencies within the national economy.
The university participated in the Second Scientific Conference on Health and Population in the New Administrative Capital, held under the theme Human Development for a Sustainable Future.
The conference provided our students with comprehensive understanding through numerous activities, interactive sessions, discussions, and workshops on diverse topics in health, population, and human development, delivered by local and international leaders, policymakers, entrepreneurs, researchers, and healthcare experts.
Under the supervision of:
Assoc. Prof. Dr. Mohamed Yasser, Director of the Pharmacy Program,
Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program,
Prof. Dr. Rasha Emad, Director of the Applied Health Sciences Technology Program,

The university also participated in the Scientific Week for Entrepreneurship held at Suez Canal University, aimed at promoting innovation and supporting entrepreneurship as part of Egypt Vision 2030 for sustainable development.
During the event, winning students in entrepreneurship competitions such as the Startup Olympics and the Egyptian Entrepreneurship League were honored, further motivating us to develop our ideas and transform them into practical projects.
Under the supervision of:
Assoc. Prof. Dr. Mohamed Yasser, Director of the Pharmacy Program,
Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program,
Dr. Mohamed Mahrous, Lecturer at the Faculty of Pharmacy,
The university concluded its participation by attending the opening events of the Tamkeen Initiative in cooperation with the Global Hope Givers Campaign at Suez Canal University.
