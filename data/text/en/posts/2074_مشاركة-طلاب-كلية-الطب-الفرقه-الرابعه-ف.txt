TITLE: Faculty of Medicine Students Participate in the Sixth Annual Mansoura Manchester Conference
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/مشاركة-طلاب-كلية-الطب-الفرقه-الرابعه-ف/
DATE: 2026-03-17T22:07:58

Under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, President of East Port Said National University,
and Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, Director of the university's Medicine and Surgery Program,
and under the supervision of Dr. Nesreen Farouk, Lecturer of Public Health and Community Medicine,
and Dr. Nehal Gamal Sharaf El-Din, Teaching Assistant of Public Health and Community Medicine,
fourth-year Faculty of Medicine students at East Port Said National University participated in the Sixth Annual Mansoura Manchester Student Research Conference with a scientific paper entitled:
"Prevalence and Risk Factors of Autism Spectrum Disorder among Preschool Children in Port Said Governorate"
(Prevalence and Risk Factors of Autism Spectrum Disorder among Preschool Children in Port Said Governorate)
This participation comes as part of the university's efforts to strengthen the applied research role of its students in addressing important health issues in society.
The university is proud of its participating students, who presented an honorable model of serious scientific research, teamwork, and academic commitment. It wishes them continued success and affirms its ongoing support for all efforts that contribute to developing students' research and innovation skills.
