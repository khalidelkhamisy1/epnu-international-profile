TITLE: The First Coordination Meeting of the Students for Egypt Family at East Port Said National University
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/students-for-egypt/
DATE: 2024-11-15T09:41:11

Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Vice President of the University, held
her first meeting with the Students for Egypt family in the presence of Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, the family coordinator.
This is the first coordination meeting of the Students for Egypt family at East Port Said National University.
nder the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, President of the University,
She thanked the family organizers and students for their efforts since the beginning of the family's work.
During the meeting, she discussed the family's future strategies and mechanisms, as well as proposed programs to be implemented in line with the university's goals and objectives and the needs and requirements of university students.
She emphasized that the university aspires for the Students for Egypt family to become one of the leading student families nationwide.
