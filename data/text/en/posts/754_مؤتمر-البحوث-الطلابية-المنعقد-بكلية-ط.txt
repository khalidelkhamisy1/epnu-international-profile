TITLE: Student Research Conference Held at Mansoura Faculty of Medicine (Manchester)
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/مؤتمر-البحوث-الطلابية-المنعقد-بكلية-ط/
DATE: 2024-10-18T11:09:55

The Faculty of Medicine at East Port Said National University participated
under the patronage of Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, and under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Rasha Anany, Supervisor of the Education and Student Affairs Sector, in the Student Research Conference held at Mansoura Faculty of Medicine (Manchester).
Two submitted research papers were accepted, and their posters were presented at the conference. More than 80 research papers from nine faculties nationwide participated. This marked East Port Said National University's first participation in this conference. He praised the efforts made by Dr. Sherine El-Khouly, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program,
and her supporting team, wishing them continued success and achievement.
