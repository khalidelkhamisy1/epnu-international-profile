TITLE: East Port Said National University Honors the President of Port Said University in Recognition of Distinguished Academic Cooperation
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/جامعة-شرق-بورسعيد-الأهلية-تكرّم-رئيس-ج/
DATE: 2026-06-29T17:43:41

As part of the strong and longstanding partnership between East Port Said National University and Port Said University, Professor Atef El-Din, President of East Port Said National University, honored Professor Sherif Youssef Saleh, President of Port Said University, by presenting him with the University's Shield during the closing ceremony of the Student Activities at the Faculty of Medicine.
The ceremony was attended by Professor Ehab El-Hanafy, Dean of the Faculty of Medicine at Port Said University, and Associate Professor Sherine El-Khouly, Director of the Medicine and Surgery Program at East Port Said National University, along with a number of academic leaders and faculty members.
This recognition reflects the University's appreciation for Professor Sherif Youssef Saleh's outstanding efforts in strengthening academic cooperation between the two universities and his continued commitment to fostering strategic partnerships and the exchange of expertise. These collaborative efforts contribute to enhancing the quality of education, advancing academic excellence, and promoting integration among higher education institutions in support of student success and institutional development.
