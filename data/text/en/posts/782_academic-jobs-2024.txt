TITLE: Academic Vacancies Announcement
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/academic-jobs-2024/
DATE: 2024-12-23T13:47:07

As part of East Port Said National University's efforts to build a rich and diverse database of distinguished academic cadres capable of contributing to the university's development, addressing staffing needs in some faculties, and supporting the educational process, examinations, and university activities, the university announces the opening of applications for faculty member and teaching assistant positions through annual contracts, full-time or part-time secondment, or appointment, in accordance with the conditions, controls, and standards stipulated in the relevant laws, regulations, and the rules governing this announcement.
For further details, please visit the following link:
https://forms.gle/18fChJi2oEEZ4bzWA
General Application Criteria:
The applicant must be an Egyptian national.
The applicant must have obtained at least a Very Good grade in the first university degree.
The applicant must have obtained at least a Good grade in the specialization.
Proficiency in spoken and written English, with the ability to communicate effectively in academic contexts.
Proficiency in using general and specialized computer applications.
Ability to work effectively as part of a team.
General Application Criteria:
Adequate academic experience in university teaching and curriculum development.
Participation in distinguished research and scientific projects at the local and international levels.
Publication of scientific research in specialized peer-reviewed international journals.
Experience in using electronic grading technologies and modern assessment and evaluation systems.
Contribution to university and community service activities.
Experience in academic advising for students.
Familiarity with academic quality assurance and planning work.
Required Application Documents:
Complete the application form using the link attached to the announcement.
A copy of certified academic certificates.
A copy of the national ID card.
An updated CV including academic and research experience.
Any additional documents supporting the application, such as training certificates, etc.
Application Method:
Applications will be received through
https://forms.gle/18fChJi2oEEZ4bzWA
all required documents must be uploaded from December 23, 2024, to January 3, 2025.
Evaluation Stages:
First Stage:
Review of submitted documents by a committee formed by the University President.
.
Second Stage:
Inviting shortlisted candidates for a personal interview and presentation.
Disclaimer:
Any misrepresentation or omission of facts relevant to the recruitment process in the application form, CV, or personal interview may result in termination of the recruitment process or contract, in addition to other legal measures.
