TITLE: Conclusion of the Military Training Course and Certificate Distribution at East Port Said National University
URL: https://epnu.edu.eg/en/ختام-الدورة-العسكرية-وتوزيع-الشهادات/
DATE: 2026-03-17T18:44:38

East Port Said National University concluded the activities of the military training course for its students, held as part of the university's commitment to instilling the values of discipline and commitment and developing teamwork, loyalty, and belonging among students.
The closing activities were attended by Prof. Dr. Atef Alam El-Din, University President, who distributed certificates to the students who successfully completed the course after they took the final examination held at the end of the training program to measure their understanding of the theoretical and practical training they had received.
The university administration affirmed that the military training course is part of an integrated vision aimed at preparing an aware generation capable of bearing responsibility and qualified to face challenges with discipline and patriotism.
The University Board of Trustees, chaired by Eng. Ibrahim Mahlab, former Prime Minister of Egypt, also extended sincere thanks and appreciation to all entities and personnel responsible for organizing and implementing the course, praising the students' commitment and discipline, which reflected an honorable image of the university's students.
The university commended the efforts of the participating students and affirmed that it will continue organizing such programs, which contribute to building students' character and enhancing their capabilities at both the academic and practical levels.
