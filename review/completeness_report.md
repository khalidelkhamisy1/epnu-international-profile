# Completeness review: EPNU International Relations Briefing

Reviewer scope: briefing `D:\AI\EPNU\EPNU_International_Relations_Briefing.md` vs. the full scraped corpus (24 pages + 100 posts, Arabic originals and rendered English). Review only; the briefing was not edited.
Method: full read of every page that carries institutional facts, plus keyword sweeps (Arabic and English) across all 100 posts: international / MoU / protocol / delegation / ambassador / scholarship / exchange / ranking / accreditation / Malaysia, Russia, Japan, China, Europe, Manchester, Huawei, foreign companies, ECTS, IELTS/TOEFL, degrees and fellowships.
Paths: Arabic `D:\AI\EPNU\data\text\ar\{pages,posts}\<ID>_*.txt`; English `D:\AI\EPNU\data\text\en_rendered\{pages,posts}\<ID>.txt`; raw/unrendered `D:\AI\EPNU\data\text\en\...`. English-site URLs are `https://epnu.edu.eg/en/<slug>/` (exact URL lines quoted below).

**Bottom line:** the briefing already captures every item in the corpus that is explicitly "international" (UPM, Russian-Arab Rectors Forum, Horizon Europe event, National Universities Council priorities, Egyptian-Japanese Initiative, NAQAAE conference, Huawei, Saudi German Hospital, Exeter/Torino staff). ## F. Assessment of the suggested slide outline (Section 8)
**Overall: sound, but it is a university profile with an IR bias, not an IR pitch.** About 40% of slides (3, 4, 10) are national/profile content and the IR evidence is thin, so the flow risks over-promising.
Suggested restructuring for an IR audience (still 12 slides):
1. **Title and one-line positioning**: keep; add the site's own pillar "Global Openness".
2. **Who we are**: 7 faculties, 13+ programs, 5,200+ students, Sinai/East Port Said, Egypt Vision 2030, opened 2024/25 (A1).
3. **Why an IR audience should care**: Suez Canal/Sinai location, English-medium instruction, ECTS/credit hours, regional assets. Pull forward from Section 7 and B5.
4. **Leadership with international credentials**: merge old slides 3 and 4 leadership content; fix the Senate wording (A2).
5. **IR strategy and mandate**: partnership goals; Ministry directives (24 Mar 2026); National Universities Council (30 Jun 2026); PSU Council (28 Jul 2026, B1). Show it as a Mar -> Jun -> Jul 2026 policy timeline.
6. **Flagship: UPM**: show "what exists vs next" (agreement signed -> webinar -> virtual meeting -> 2nd edition announced -> exchange). Add "mobility not yet reported".
7. **Networks and forums**: Russian-Arab Rectors, Horizon Europe, Egyptian-Japanese initiative, NAQAAE. Add a small region map (Asia: Malaysia/Japan; Europe: EU/Italy; Eurasia/Arab: Russia-Arab; Africa: none yet).
8. **International-standard academics**: English medium, credit hours/ECTS, English transcripts, IGCSE/foreign certificates (with A4 nuance), non-degree and auditor registration, equivalency rules, GAHAR (B5).
9. **Global industry and academy partners**: Huawei ICT Academy, Saudi German Hospital, Pharmacy industry partners (B6), Italian ambassador/Zohr (B3, labelled as attended).
10. **Staff and students on the international stage**: Exeter/Torino/Newton-Mosharafa; ICIAAI, ITC-Egypt, Ain Shams, Suez Canal 2nd place, ECPC.
11. **National partner foundation**: PSU (23 papers), ITI, Armed Forces, 57357, Arish, Suez Canal Authority: one compact slide; context only.
12. **Roadmap and asks**: IR Office, more partners (Russia/CIS, Japan, EU), joint/dual degrees, international scholarships, faculty recruitment beyond Egyptian nationals; end with a concrete call to action (the "Partnerships and cooperation" contact route).
Other structural suggestions:
- Put an **"evidence status" label** on every IR claim (signed / event participation / invitation only / announced objective). Move caveats (bulk-posted 8 Jul 2026 dates; 29 Apr webinar unconfirmed; 57357 MoU proposed) into speaker notes, not only Section 6.
- Add a **timeline** (Oct 2024 first on-campus Board meeting -> Mar 2026 Board 10th -> Mar/Jun/Jul 2026 policy signals -> Apr-Jul 2026 UPM and forums).
- Prepare an honest answer to "where are the signed international MoUs?": one international agreement (UPM) is publicly documented; the 2026 national policy is accelerating this.
- Section 7 gaps are valid; add: (a) no foreign-faculty hiring path (Egyptian nationality required, posts 782/5477), (b) no TOEFL/IELTS minimum published, (c) 2026-27 intake limited to Egyptian-type certificates, (d) no IR Office page, (e) no mobility reports.
