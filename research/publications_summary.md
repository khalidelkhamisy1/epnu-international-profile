## Caveats and likely undercounting

- **Undercount risks:** indexing lag (2026 items still arriving); inconsistent, truncated or wrong strings ("East Port Said University", "Port Said National"); missing affiliations in metadata; staff who publish only under Port Said University or their other home institution. The true figure is probably higher than 81.
- **Overcount risks:** one EPNU-affiliated co-author is enough to qualify a paper, so many papers may be mainly Port Said University work with EPNU as a secondary affiliation. Dates reflect OpenAlex publication year.
- Co-author countries rely on OpenAlex institution matching and are a lower bound; field labels are automatic and imperfect.
- Two records (Pharmaceuticals 19(10) 1531; Drug Development Research 70364) were found only via Crossref / Europe PMC.
