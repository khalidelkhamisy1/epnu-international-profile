# EPNU research publications and research activity

Compiled 2026-10-14. Read-only research. All text in English.

## 1. Bottom line

- **No public source states "50+ international papers".** The only published count is **23 papers (Pharmacy, 2024-2025)**. The 50+ figure is NOT confirmed by any statement I could find, in local data, news or web search.
- **However, the figure is supported by independent bibliographic evidence.** A PubMed affiliation search for "East Port Said National University" returns **56 records** (searched 2026-10-14; details in section 4). That is about 52 research items once two correction notices and the occasional review article are discounted. So "50+ papers" is plausible and verifiable, but only as a PubMed/biomedical count. It is not an official university statement.
- **Scopus and Google Scholar could not be checked directly.** Scopus needs a login. No Google Scholar or ResearchGate institution page was found. OpenAlex returned a rate-limit error (429), so only a first fuzzy query was possible, and it is unusable for counts.
- Almost all the evidence is Faculty of Pharmacy (plus Applied Health Sciences, one Medicine item, one Engineering item). There is no evidence of papers from Computer Science, Business or Arts.

## 4. Independent evidence: PubMed affiliation search

Method: NCBI E-utilities, query `"East Port Said National University"[ad]`, run 2026-10-14. Result: **56 records** (publication-year breakdown: 2024 = 4; 2025 = 22; 2026 = 30, so 2026 is partial). Two of the 56 are correction notices (PMIDs 41813550 and 42402494). I verified the raw affiliation strings: 54 name EPNU as exact "East Port Said National University", and the remaining 2 (PMIDs 40043386, 42616559) name "East Port-Said National University" with a hyphen. So **all 56 genuinely name EPNU**. Subtract 2 corrections gives **54 distinct articles**; one more is a review-type article (several PMIDs are narrative reviews), so call it "about 50+ research articles". The first EPNU-affiliated paper is dated 2024 (Oct-Dec 2024, consistent with the university starting teaching in 2024-2025).

Caveats:
- PubMed covers biomedical journals only. It misses engineering, business and computing papers. Scopus would likely give a higher number, but I could not query it.
- Many papers have dual PSU/EPNU affiliations (e.g. Gaafar A, Elmaaty AA); EPNU is listed alongside Port Said University.
- Affiliation spellings vary ("East Port Said National University", "East Port-Said", "Port Said National University"), so counts depend on search wording.
- C1 reports 23 papers for AY 2024-2025 (Pharmacy only) while PubMed shows 26 records dated 2024-2025. The numbers are consistent in magnitude.

Faculties represented: Pharmacy (large majority; Medicinal Chemistry, Pharmacology & Toxicology, Pharmacy Practice, Biochemistry, Pharmaceutics, Pharmacognosy), Applied Health Sciences (Khirallah SM, Kamel IS: 6 items), Medicine (1 item, Elkholy SE, PMID 41401657), Engineering (1 item, Elsakka MM, Scientific Reports).

## 5. Recommended wording

**Is the 50+ figure confirmed by a public source?** Not as a statement. No university, ministry or press source says "50+". It is **supportable from a public bibliographic database** (PubMed: 56 records, about 54 articles, as of 2026-10-14), but that database is biomedical only and a different count method may give a different number. The user's figure should be cited as **"university data"** unless the user can supply the university's own list or a Scopus export.

Safe slide wordings, from strongest to weakest evidence:

1. **Preferred (verifiable, conservative):** "50+ research articles in international peer-reviewed journals since 2024 (PubMed-indexed, EPNU affiliation)." Footnote: "PubMed affiliation search, October 2026."
2. **If the user wants the university's figure:** "50+ international publications since founding (source: University data, 2026)." Keep "University data" in the footnote and have the user confirm the figure and cutoff date with the Research office.
3. **Only fully published number, Pharmacy:** "23 papers published in 2024-2025 in the Faculty of Pharmacy with Port Said University, 20 by faculty in international journals (Q1 to Q3)." (Source C1.)
4. **Avoid:** "50+ Scopus-indexed papers" (Scopus not checked); "all Q1"; "published in international journals by EPNU alone" (many are joint with PSU); "fifth paper" claims; any statement implying all faculties publish.

Also advisable on a slide: add "Student research: 11 papers presented at national conferences in 2024-2026" (2 Mansoura + 2 Ain Shams + 7 Suez Canal), which is verified by C4 to C6.
