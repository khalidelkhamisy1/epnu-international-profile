## A. Risk and reputation (read before presenting)

### A1. Medicine intake crisis, Aug-Oct 2026 [NOTES, brief if asked]
- **Fact:** The Supreme Council of Private (Ahliya) Universities set a cap of **300 students** for EPNU's Faculty of Medicine about one week before the semester started. EPNU had admitted and collected fees from about **660 students**, so about **360** were told around 22 Aug / early Sept to withdraw their fees. Minimum score for Medicine was reported as **82%**.
- **Figures differ by source:** 85 affected (Ministry/Akhbar El-Youm), 35-85 (President, 9 Sep), 360 (Sada El-Balad, Egypt Telegraph), and a "50% cut" headline. Use "hundreds" or give a range.
- **Ministry response (spokesperson Adel Abdel Ghaffar; Minister Dr. Abdelaziz Konsowa):** four alternatives were offered:
  - stay at EPNU's Faculty of Pharmacy;
  - transfer to Dentistry or Physical Therapy at Suez Canal University or at Ismailia National University (sources conflict on which);
  - Medicine at King Salman International University (El-Tor).
  - Fees can be withdrawn in full or the difference refunded. The Ministry reported on 16 Sept that about 85 students were still unresolved.
- **Stated cause:** only reported by an MP (Ahmed Farghaly): the university hospital was not ready. Official reasons were not published.
- **Dates:** 9 Sep 2026 (Sada El-Balad); 16 Sep 2026 (Al Mal); 11 Oct 2026 (Egypt Telegraph); Akhbar El-Youm item undated.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.elbalad.news/7100911
  - https://see-edu.news/2026/09/09/جامعة-شرق-بورسعيد-3
  - https://almalnews.com/2135887/4-بدائل-في-أزمة-جامعة-شرق-بورسعيد/
  - https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/285930
  - https://m.akhbarelyom.com/news/NewDetails/4879921/1/التعليم-العالي-حل-مشكلة-طلاب-جامعة-شرق-ب
- **Confidence:** high that it happened; low-medium on numbers.
- **Why it matters:**
  - A visiting delegation may have read about it.
  - It shows Medicine intake is **capped by the Supreme Council (about 300)** and is **tied to teaching-hospital readiness**. Do not promise Medicine places or exchange numbers beyond that.
  - Keep a neutral line ready: "Medicine intake follows Supreme Council capacity limits."

## B. Fees, admission and student numbers

### B1. Tuition fees 2026-27 for Egyptian students [SLIDE for an admissions or "why EPNU" slide; use fees as "approximate, per year"]
- Base annual fee (EGP): Medicine **149,000**; Pharmacy **99,000**; Engineering **72,000**; Computer Science & IT **61,000**; Applied Health Sciences **50,000**; Business Sciences **40,000**; Arts **40,000**.
- A **3% administrative fee** is added. Payment is in **two equal instalments**, online only. Applications opened **Mon 3 Aug 2026**.
- Minimum scores reported (by filgamaa.com, unofficial): Medicine 82%, Pharmacy 70%, Engineering 65%. Treat these as unconfirmed.
- **Date:** 4 Aug 2026.
- **Sources:**
  - https://www.egypttelegraph.com/article/267329/تنسيق-جامعة-شرق-بورسعيد-الأهلية-2026-المصروفات
  - https://filgamaa.com/جامعة-شرق-بورسعيد-الأهلية-2026-2027/
- **Confidence:** medium-high for fees (two outlets agree on Medicine and Pharmacy; the university site had no figures in what was retrieved); low for scores.
- **Why it matters:** answers the question "what does it cost?". The figures are for Egyptians. They are NOT international fees.
- **Earlier years:** health sciences were reported at about EGP 35,000 a year in 2025-26 (filgamaa.com). Fees rose by about 43% for that faculty. Medium confidence.

### B2. International (wafedeen) fees [NOT FOUND]
- No published fee schedule for international students was found. The Egyptian national wafedeen platform fee reported for 2023-24 was about USD 170 (an unrelated, system-wide fee).
- **Why it matters:** this is the biggest gap for an IR audience. Do not quote any figure. Ask the admissions office.

### B3. Study-in-Egypt route for international students [NOTES]
- A third-party agency page (study-in-egy.org, updated 29 Jan 2026) describes the route: apply on the Study in Egypt platform, authenticate documents through the Egyptian embassy and foreign ministry, receive preliminary approval, pay fees through the platform, then get the enrolment card. Passport required.
- It claims the medical programmes are accredited by the Supreme Council. No source is given for that claim.
- **Source:** https://study-in-egy.org/?p=2472
- **Confidence:** low. It is a promotional agency page, **not an official channel**. The university says it does not deal with agents.
- **Why it matters:** the page lists EPNU as admitting foreign students, but EPNU's own 2026-27 intake call lists only Egyptian-type certificates. Do not claim EPNU is on Study in Egypt until confirmed.

### B4. Student numbers [NOTES]
- No official annual enrolment series was found. Data points:
  - Medicine 2025-26 intake was **660**, "the same as the previous year" (Sada El-Balad, 9 Sep 2026). This suggests 660 Medicine admissions in 2024-25 too.
  - A Wikipedia and Wikidata listing says "founded 2021". The President says studies began **2024-25**; one source says 2022/23. The conflict is not resolved.
  - A Dostor article (Feb 2025) and the Al Mal article (May 2024) give no student totals.
- **Confidence:** medium.
- **Why it matters:** the "5,200+ students" website figure is unaudited. A 660-student Medicine cohort for two years plus the other faculties makes it plausible.

## C. Campus, cost and development context

### C1. Cost figures are inconsistent across sources [NOTES, update the briefing]
- **Al Mal (20 May 2024):** estimated EGP **5 billion**, 44 feddans.
- **Al-Watan (25 Apr 2026, citing the Minister):** about EGP **4.6 billion** for the first phase, construction and equipment.
- **Another source:** EGP 3.7 bn.
- The briefing's EGP 3.5 bn comes from a Ministry report shared on Facebook.
- **Sources:**
  - https://almalnews.com/1769092/5-مليارات-جنيه-التكاليف-الاستثمارية-لإنشاء-جامعة/
  - https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8272442
- **Confidence:** high that figures differ.
- **Why it matters:** the safest slide wording is "billions of EGP invested, 44-feddan campus". The most recent official (Minister, April 2026) figure is **EGP 4.6 bn**.

### C2. Original planned structure: 8 colleges, 23 programs, 3 sectors [SLIDE as "growth plan"]
- At the May 2024 Board meeting the plan was **8 colleges (adding Dentistry)** in three sectors: engineering and physical sciences; medicine and biomedical sciences; arts and humanities. **23 programs** were planned.
- Buildings listed: administration building, 2 engineering buildings, medicine, pharmacy and dentistry buildings, a laboratories building, 2 workshop buildings, and **6 future buildings**.
- Today there are 7 faculties (Dentistry is not offered).
- **Date:** 20 May 2024 (2nd Board meeting, chaired by Eng. Ibrahim Mahlab; Port Said Governor Adel El-Ghadban and Education Minister Reda Hegazy attended).
- **Sources:**
  - https://elbalad.news/6207125
  - https://almalnews.com/1769092/5-مليارات-جنيه-التكاليف-الاستثمارية-لإنشاء-جامعة/
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Why it matters:** gives a credible growth roadmap. It also corrects the Board timeline: the briefing lists the 1st meeting as Oct 2024, but a **2nd meeting was reported in May 2024**, so the dating in the briefing needs checking.

### C3. Link to the Suez Canal Economic Zone and the East Port Said industrial zone [SLIDE]
- Port Said Governor Lt. Gen. Mohab Habashy said the university serves the needs of national projects in the **Suez Canal Economic Zone** and will draw students from neighbouring governorates.
- Dostor says the campus lies near the **industrial zone and the East Port Said port**.
- **Date:** 10 Feb 2025.
- **Source:** https://www.dostor.org/4959251
- **Confidence:** high (a governor's statement as reported by the press). There is no formal agreement with SCZone, so say "serves the needs of" and not "partners with".
- **Why it matters:** the SCZone and port location is the best regional selling point for the IR deck.

### C4. Programme additions and twinning plan [SLIDE, with caution]
- In Feb 2025 the President announced three new programmes (**AI, Mechatronics, Construction & Building Engineering**).
- He also said the university was **seeking a twinning protocol with an established Egyptian university that has foreign-university partnerships**, to offer study abroad and **dual degrees**.
- **Date:** 10 Feb 2025.
- **Source:** https://www.dostor.org/4959251
- **Confidence:** high that it was announced; no evidence it has been signed.
- **Why it matters:** confirms the dual-degree intention. It is an **indirect route** (through a partner Egyptian university), not a foreign MoU. Present it as a plan.

### C5. Sibling university: East Port Said Technological University [NOTES]
- A separate institution, **East Port Said Technological University**, costs about EGP 808 million. Its acting president is Dr. Heba Abdel-Ati. It expects its first graduating class at the end of the 2025-26 academic year.
- **Date:** 22 Apr 2026.
- **Source:** https://www.dostor.org/5520599 (Dostor, 22 Apr 2026)
- **Confidence:** medium-high.
- **Why it matters:** avoid confusing the two when talking to partners. EPNU is the Ahliya one.

### C6. University hospital [NOTES]
- No public information on an EPNU teaching hospital (beds or opening date) was found. The only hint is an MP's claim that the university hospital was not ready, which was linked to the Medicine cap (see A1). Port Said University's new university hospital opened **26 Dec 2025** (about EGP 1 bn); the EPNU President attended (this is already in the briefing).
- **Why it matters:** the Medicine clinical pathway depends on partner hospitals (PSU, 57357, Ahl Masr). Say "clinical training through partner hospitals".

## D. National context and ministry statements

### D1. Port Said University as "strategic partner", 4 Aug 2026 [SLIDE, supports the national partnership slide]
- Port Said University President Prof. Sherif Saleh met the EPNU President and the EPNU programme directors at Port Said University.
- Saleh called EPNU a "strategic partner" and promised academic and administrative expertise. EPNU's President called the cooperation "a core pillar" of the university's launch. Topics: execution plans for academic programmes, resources sharing, readiness to receive students.
- **Source:** https://www.dostor.org/5657119 (Al-Dostor, 4 Aug 2026)
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Why it matters:** this is the cleanest statement of institutional backing. It also confirms that PSU staff and expertise carry the early programmes.

### D2. Ministry statements, 25 Apr 2026 [SLIDE for context]
- Minister Dr. Abdelaziz Konsowa said higher-education investments in Sinai and the Canal cities total about EGP **25.6 bn**. EPNU's President called it a fourth-generation university with advanced infrastructure. The Ministry spokesperson said Egypt now has **32 private (Ahliya) universities** emphasising academic and industrial partnerships.
- **Source:** https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8272442
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Why it matters:** places EPNU in a state programme that also includes King Salman International University (EGP 10.5 bn), New Ismailia Ahliya University (EGP 4.5 bn), Suez University Abu Redis and Arish University.

### D3. Port Said University Pharmacy graduation, 2 Aug 2026 [NOTES]
- The heads of EPNU and East Port Said Technological University attended Port Said University's Pharmacy graduation (126 graduates, first Clinical Pharmacy cohort, Governor Ibrahim Abu Leimon present).
- **Source:** https://www.elbalad.news/7058965
- **Confidence:** high.
- **Why it matters:** not an EPNU graduation. **No EPNU cohort has graduated yet** (the first cohort started 2024-25).
