## Slides judged clean

Slide 5 has no overflow, crop or alignment problems; the portrait is well framed. Titles on all content slides are consistent in position and size, the logo never collides with a title, and no title has a split word or wraps to a second line.

## Top 5 design improvements

1. **Normalize the grid and margins.** Use one content frame of x 0.6 to 12.73 in and y 1.75 to 6.7 in, with 0.3 in gutters. This fixes slides 2, 3, 6, 12, 13, 15 and 16, where right edges (11.98, 12.65, 12.73, 12.88, 13.04 in) and bottoms differ.
2. **Fill cards properly.** Many cards (3, 8, 9, 11, 14) have about 1 in of empty space at the bottom. Cut card heights and give the space to larger type (12 to 14 pt), which also fixes the small-text issue. The cramped cards (6, 17) get the padding they need.
3. **Upgrade the imagery.** Replace the screenshot-like or low-resolution pictures (webinar on 12, hospital on 14, meeting strip on 16, lab on 9) with clean landscape photos, add short captions, and use consistent rounded corners. Remove third-party brand and joke props (15).
4. **Make the divider slides premium.** Add a faint campus photo with a navy gradient, or a large outline numeral, with the same logo placement as content slides.
5. **Polish the typography.** Use a fixed 2-line heading zone or one-line headings in card grids, curly quotes, non-breaking hyphens ("ECTS-based", "Cross-Border"), and title breaks that avoid orphans (slide 1). Strengthen the closing slide with a named contact, email and QR code.
