### Notes:
Many partners ask whether we are ready to work internationally. Our regulations already contain the building blocks. Teaching is in English. We use credit hours, where a semester's workload of 750 hours is about 30 ECTS credits, and Medicine follows ECTS. Our rules allow non-degree registration, auditor registration, and transfer with course equivalency. Graduation certificates are issued in Arabic and English. Admission rules recognize routes including British IGCSE, the Nile International Certificate and recognized foreign certificates, and accept a passport as ID. We also host a Huawei ICT Academy, linked to our AI and machine learning program.

CAVEATS:
- The 2026-27 intake call lists only Egyptian Thanaweya Amma, Al-Azhar, STEM and Nile Schools certificates, and says the university does not deal with agents. Do not promise international intake this year.
- Mobility-ready rules exist on paper; no exchange or credit transfer with a foreign university has been reported.
- Non-native speakers of Arabic must show Arabic proficiency; no minimum English score is published.
- Do not describe the Huawei Academy as a signed agreement; the briefing only describes training offered.

LIKELY QUESTION: Can international students apply this year?
ANSWER: Our admission rules provide for foreign certificates, but the 2026-27 intake call lists only Egyptian-type certificates, so an international admissions track is something we would like to develop with partners.

### Notes:
